## Chosen approach: Signal / Field Notes

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial brutalism fused with Swiss information design and cinematic product-interface art direction.

### Core Principles
1. Make technical work feel tangible through diagrams, systems language, and material contrast.
2. Use oversized typography and deliberate negative space to create a confident reading rhythm.
3. Treat orange as a signal, not decoration: it marks action, active states, and important transitions.
4. Keep motion quiet, physical, and useful so recruiters can scan without losing the immersive quality.

### Color Philosophy
The base is near-black graphite so the portfolio feels like a focused engineering workbench. Warm white creates paper-like reading surfaces and gives long-form content a calm editorial register. Electric orange is reserved for signal states, links, active nodes, and calls to action; the scarcity makes each accent feel intentional and ownable.

### Layout Paradigm
A sequence of oversized rounded panels and offset content rails rather than a centered card grid. The hero uses an asymmetric split between editorial copy and an interactive technical object. Projects alternate panel density and image-to-copy ratios. Timeline content follows a vertical signal trace. Contact is a single dramatic closing panel.

### Signature Elements
1. Orange calibration marks, signal dots, and thin diagram lines used as section punctuation.
2. Rounded black or paper panels with clipped crop corners and subtle technical texture.
3. A recurring RS signal mark and node language connecting the hero, project visuals, skills map, and footer.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should feel like inspecting a system: hover reveals a state, focus makes a path visible, and scrolling advances the narrative. Buttons respond with small magnetic drift and scale rather than exaggerated bounce. Motion should clarify hierarchy and preserve the page's calm.

### Animation
Use opacity and transform for reveal sequences, 40–70ms stagger between grouped items, subtle parallax on visual panels, light pointer-following on the hero scene, and restrained 3D tilt on project cards. Keep transitions generally under 500ms, pause decorative motion when offscreen where possible, and disable non-essential animation under prefers-reduced-motion. Touch devices receive a static/fallback technical composition.

### Typography System
Use Space Grotesk for display headings and IBM Plex Mono for metadata, labels, and technical annotations. Use a readable modern sans-serif system stack for body copy. Headings are large, tightly tracked, and frequently sentence-case or uppercase only where the editorial label calls for it. Metadata remains compact, uppercase, and letter-spaced.

### Brand Essence
A resume-backed engineering portfolio for recruiters and teams looking for practical Python, backend, QA, AI/ML, and computer-vision capability; different because it explains the systems behind the work instead of only displaying outcomes.

Personality: precise, curious, grounded.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and observant. CTAs are specific and low-pressure. Microcopy sounds like a field note rather than a sales pitch.

Example lines:
- "Reliable systems, then the questions that make them smarter."
- "Inspect the work →"

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the generated text-free RS engineering mark as the compact brand symbol. Pair it with a small monospaced `RS / ENGINEERING` lockup rather than relying on a default wordmark font.

### Signature Brand Color
Electric orange: `#FF5A1F`, used as the active signal color across the interface.
